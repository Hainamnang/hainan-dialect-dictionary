## Terminology Rule

Use the Thai term "ไฮ้หน่ำ" only.

Do not use the Thai term "ไหหลำ" anywhere in this project, including UI text, menu labels, descriptions, placeholders, metadata, comments, documentation, or future generated code.

If referring to the Hainanese language/dialect in Thai, always write "ไฮ้หน่ำ".
